🔟 Adding Whole Tens

Count in $+10$ hops like Ada & Cyrus with hockey sticks ($25 + 30 = 55$)!

Textbook pp. 71–72
👧🏽
💭 Ada's Estimation Secret: "Both numbers are big! 30 + 30 = 60, so I estimate we have about 60 hockey sticks!"
💡 Key Observation: Whole tens have 0 ones. So the ones column NEVER changes when you add whole tens!
💬 Parent & Teacher Discussion Prompts (Tap to open)

🔹 Ask: "Look at 25 + 30. Does the 5 change? Why not? (5 + 0 = 5!)."

🔹 Ask: "What is 2 tens + 3 tens? (5 tens!). So 25 + 30 = 55!"

🔹 Hopping Rule: Hop by 10s: 25 ➔ 35 ➔ 45 ➔ 55!

⚡ Double-Split & Column Addition

Break both numbers into tens and ones bonds as in Workbook p. 53 & Textbook p. 73!

Workbook 4 (p. 53)
✏️ Ada's Erasers Story: Ada's sister has 21 erasers. Ada has 17 erasers more than her sister. How many erasers does Ada have? $21 + 17 = 38$!
💬 Parent & Teacher Discussion Prompts (Tap to open)

🔹 Ask: "Should we count on using a number line for 21 + 17? Why or why not?" (Textbook p. 73 Maths Talk: No, because hopping 17 times takes too long! Place value splitting is much faster!).

🔹 Step 1: Split both numbers: $21 \to (20 + 1)$ and $17 \to (10 + 7)$.

🔹 Step 2: Add ones: $1 + 7 = 8$. Add tens: $20 + 10 = 30$.

🔹 Step 3: Combine tens and ones: $30 + 8 = 38$!
