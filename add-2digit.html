⚡ 2-Digit Split Strategy

Decompose both numbers into tens and ones, add tens first, ones second, then combine!

Mental Math Method
💡 Mental Math Secret: Split each number into Tens and Ones. Adding $30 + 20$ is easy, and adding $5 + 4$ is easy!
35 + 24 = 59
1
Split both numbers into Tens and Ones:
Split 35 ➔ (30 + 5) • Split 24 ➔ (20 + 4)
2
Add the Tens:
30 + 20 = 50
3
Add the Ones:
5 + 4 = 9
4
Combine the Tens and Ones:
50 + 9 = 59 🎉
