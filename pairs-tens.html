🔍 The 10× Magic Lens (Bridge to Tens)

Vera's discovery from textbook p.67 — Adding whole tens is just adding ones with a 10× Lens!

Textbook p.67 • Concept Builder
💬 Parent & Teacher Discussion Prompts (Tap to open)

🔹 Ask: "What is $6 + 2$? (Answer: $8$)." Now switch the lens to 10×: "What is $60 + 20$?" ($80$!).

🔹 Ask: "Look at the ones place in $60$ and $20$. Why is it still $0$?" (Because we only added whole tens, zero ones were added!).

🔹 Insight: If a student knows their facts up to 10, they already know all their facts up to 100.

🔬 Under the 10× Lens: 6 tens + 2 tens = 8 tens (60 + 20 = 80)
