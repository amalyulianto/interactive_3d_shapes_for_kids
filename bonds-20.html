One number bond tells 4 related stories: 2 for adding and 2 for taking away!

Textbook p.64 • Maths Talk
💬 Parent & Teacher Discussion Prompts (Tap to open)

🔹 Ask: "If we turn the bag around, does the total number of balls change?" (Commutative Property: $17 + 3 = 3 + 17$).

🔹 Ask: "If 20 balls fill the bag, and we take out 3, how many must be left inside?" ($20 - 3 = 17$).

🔹 Key Understanding: Addition and subtraction are partners. Kids don't need to count backwards from 20—they just remember the number bond!
